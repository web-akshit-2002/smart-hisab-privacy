Privacy Policy for Smart Hisab

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 | Last Updated: November 1, 2026

🔒 100% Offline & Private: Smart Hisab does not request internet permission and never transmits your personal financial data to external servers.

1. Overview & Core Privacy Guarantee

Smart Hisab ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from the ground up with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. We believe your financial records, income, daily expenses, and personal khata dues are strictly your private business.

The application does not request or possess the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android Manifest. The app does not upload or sync your records to our servers. If you choose to export records, share a reminder, or contact support, Android may open another app to complete the action you requested. That service handles the information you choose to share under its own privacy policy.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All data entered into the app is stored strictly within a private, on-device SQLite database managed by Android Room:

  • Transaction Records: Expense and income entries, dates, category tags, payment modes (Cash, Bank, UPI), and notes.
  • Monthly Budgets: Spending limits configured for each category.
  • Personal Khata (Udhar / Len-Den): Contact names and balances representing money you need to receive (Lena) or pay (Dena).
  • App Settings: Language preference (English, Gujarati, Hindi) and PIN lock settings.

You retain 100% ownership and control of this data. If you uninstall the app or clear app data in Android Settings, this local data will be deleted.

3. System Permissions Used

Smart Hisab requests only the bare minimum system permissions necessary to operate selected user-facing features:

  • Biometric / Fingerprint (USE_BIOMETRIC): Optional. Used strictly by your device's operating system to unlock the application if you enable App Lock. Biometric credentials never leave your device hardware.
  • File export: When you choose to export records, the app creates a CSV file and lets you select a destination or sharing app using Android.
  • Notifications and vibration: Used for on-device alerts where enabled.
  • SMS and call logs: The version 4 Google Play build does not request SMS or call log permissions and does not automatically read incoming bank SMS messages.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Smart Hisab contains:

  • Zero In-App Ads: No AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, or banner/interstitial advertising networks.
  • Zero Behavioral Tracking: No third-party marketing, analytics, or behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • No Financial Cross-Selling: No promotion of personal loans, credit cards, or third-party financial products.

5. Data Export & Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your physical phone, you have complete power over data deletion:

  • You can delete individual entries or reset records inside the app settings.
  • You can export a complete CSV file of your transactions before changing devices.
  • Clearing the app's data in Android Settings permanently wipes all local records.
